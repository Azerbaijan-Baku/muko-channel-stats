Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Who uses this app

Muko Channel Stats is a personal tool used only by its owner for the owner's own creator accounts. No other people sign in to it.

What it reads

After the account owner signs in with TikTok and allows access, the app reads basic profile information (display name, avatar), account statistics (follower, like and video counts) and the list of the owner's public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts.

How it is stored

The numbers are saved on the owner's own computer to draw a private dashboard. Access tokens are kept only in a local file on that computer.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions. Deleting the local files removes all stored data.
